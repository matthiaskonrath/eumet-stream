**When this matters.** Publishing the *source* triggers nothing: the crates are
not redistributed here, Cargo fetches them. Distributing a *compiled binary*
does, because the dependencies are linked into it, and both MIT and Apache-2.0
require their notices to travel with the copies. This file is what you ship
